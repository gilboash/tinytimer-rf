Signal strength at the base (dBm)

One point per packet heard from the gate. Pass packets are filled, status packets hollow.

Signal-to-noise (dB)

SF7 demodulates down to about −7.5 dB; below roughly 0 dB expect lost packets.

Noise: quads off or far away, press, wait ~7 s. Peak: press, then hold a powered quad in the gate (or fly through) for about 4 s — do each pilot in turn, or all together if they fly at once. Suggested thresholds sit 35% / 60% of the way from noise to peak. Settings are sent over LoRa and retried until the gate confirms; “Gate has” shows what the gate last reported (a status every 15 s). Channel off = pilot not scanned.

Live levels over LoRa

The gate sends a sample about every 0.4 s while this is on (the highest level since the last sample is plotted, so short spikes show). It stops by itself after a minute unless this page keeps it going. It uses a few percent of the radio's duty-cycle budget, so use it to tune thresholds and then switch it off. The selected pilot's enter / exit thresholds are drawn dashed.
